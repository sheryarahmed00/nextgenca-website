Services

Outsourcing for accountancy firms

Extra capacity for your practice in busy season: UK-qualified work delivered under your name, reviewed by a Chartered Accountant.

What is included

  • Bookkeeping on your clients' software
  • Year-end accounts preparation with working papers
  • White-label payroll bureau
  • Management accounts packs
  • Self Assessment and corporation tax return preparation
  • One trial job so you can judge the quality first

Fees

Bookkeepingfrom £12 per hour
Year-end accounts preparationfrom £90 per job
Payroll bureau, white labelfrom £3 per payslip
Management accounts packfrom £80 per pack

Prices are starting fees. Your quote is fixed for the work described in your engagement letter.

How it works

  • We agree scope, turnaround and how files are shared, under a confidentiality agreement.
  • You send the job; we prepare it on your software and templates.
  • You review and sign off. Your client deals only with you.

Dates worth knowing

  • Self Assessment peak: November to 31 January.
  • Company year ends cluster around 31 March and 31 December, with filing 9 months later.
  • We agree turnaround times with you before peak season starts.

Rules and deadlines are set by HMRC and Companies House and can change. We confirm what applies to you before any work starts.

Questions

Will you contact our clients?

No. We work in the background and all client contact stays with your firm.

Who does the work?

Work is prepared and reviewed under Sheryar Ahmed ACA FCCA, a member of both ICAEW and ACCA.

Can we start small?

Yes. You can start with one trial job and then agree a regular arrangement if it works for you.
