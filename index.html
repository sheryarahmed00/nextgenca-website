Over 100,000 companies a year file their Company Tax Return more than 12 months late: what directors should know

HMRC says more than 100,000 companies a year file their Company Tax Return over a year late. Returns are due even when there is no tax to pay, and directors stay legally responsible.

Company cars and medical cover move into payroll from 6 April 2027: what employers need to do

From 6 April 2027 company cars, car fuel, vans, van fuel and medical benefits leave employees' tax codes and must be reported and taxed through payroll in real time, with Class 1A NICs paid through payroll too.

Moved to Making Tax Digital in April 2026? Your 31 January 2027 payments may show in two accounts

HMRC is writing to people who joined Making Tax Digital for Income Tax from 6 April 2026. The amounts due on 31 January 2027 may be split between your Self Assessment statement and your MTD account.

    Questions

    Before you ask

    Can I switch from my current accountant?

    Yes. We write to your current accountant for the handover information. You do not need to manage the move yourself.

    Do you work with clients outside Luton and London?

    Yes. Everything is done online, so we act for clients anywhere in the UK and abroad.

    Are the prices really fixed?

    Your quote is fixed for the work described in your engagement letter. If the work changes, we agree a new fee with you first.

    My books are behind or I have missed a deadline. Can you help?

    Yes. Tell us what is outstanding and we will set out what to file first and what any penalties are likely to be.

    Which software do you use?

    We work with Xero, QuickBooks, Sage and FreeAgent, and can set one up for you if you have none.
